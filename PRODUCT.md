# Yahua Ink Website Product Context

## Product and platform

- Public, static, multilingual B2B marketing website for Yahua (Fujian) Ink Technology Co., Ltd.
- Current stack: plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript with downloadable PDF resources.
- Existing languages: English, Simplified Chinese, and Russian.
- Planned market readiness: Arabic and right-to-left layout support. This is an inference from the owner's stated interest in future Middle East expansion, not a claim of current operations there.

## Primary users

- Flexible-packaging printers, converters, and production teams comparing ink systems.
- Procurement and technical reviewers who need to understand substrates, printing methods, production capability, and available compliance documents.
- Overseas partners who need concise, credible information before beginning a technical conversation.

## Core purpose

Help a qualified buyer quickly answer four questions:

1. What does Yahua make?
2. Which ZXA series fits my substrate and printing method?
3. Can Yahua support production at meaningful scale?
4. Where can I inspect technical and compliance documents?

## Brand and business facts to preserve

- Company: Yahua (Fujian) Ink Technology Co., Ltd., based in Changtai District, Zhangzhou.
- Business: R&D, production, and marketing of printing inks for flexible packaging.
- Portfolio: gravure surface inks, flexographic surface inks, gravure reverse-printing lamination inks, special functional inks, and traditional solvent-based printing inks.
- Stated production footprint: more than 30,000 m².
- Stated annual capacity: more than 30,000 tons.
- Brand values: “Health, Green, Safety.”
- The supplied company brief states that Yahua has a dedicated R&D team, specialised laboratories, advanced testing instruments, and long-term cooperation with overseas research institutions.
- Stated recognition includes China Famous Brand, Fujian Famous Science and Technology Enterprise, Zhangzhou Top 10 Cultural Enterprises, and ISO/CNAB certification references.
- Product series in scope: ZXA-59, ZXA-63, ZXA-68, ZXA-78, and ZXA-88.

## Product truth

- ZXA-59: water-based gravure ink for paper substrates; suited to coated, light-coated, and sheet paper.
- ZXA-63: water-based gravure ink for PVC and PETG shrink films.
- ZXA-68: gravure surface ink for PE, OPP, CPP, PET, pearlescent film, metallized film, and aluminium foil.
- ZXA-78: water-based flexographic surface ink for paper and multiple plastic films; customisation is stated as available.
- ZXA-88: gravure reverse-printing lamination ink for PE, OPP, and PET.

## Experience principles

- Look like a specialist industrial materials company, not a generic template or a lifestyle brand.
- Let ink behavior, print registration, drawdown tests, film layers, and substrate notation form the visual language.
- Lead with concise evidence and product fit; keep marketing claims restrained and traceable to supplied material.
- Keep the company mark visible, but do not let the legacy square artwork dictate the full page composition.
- Support keyboard use, reduced motion, mobile layouts, and RTL reading order.
