An ink company built around the print line.

Yahua (Fujian) Ink Technology Co., Ltd. brings R&D, production, and marketing together in Changtai District, Zhangzhou.

From formulation to supply, the work stays connected.

The portfolio spans gravure surface inks, flexographic surface inks, gravure reverse-printing lamination inks, special functional inks, and traditional solvent-based printing inks for flexible packaging.

A dedicated R&D team, specialised laboratories, advanced testing equipment, and long-term collaboration with overseas research institutions support ongoing formulation work.
